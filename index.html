Improving

Positions rose on most tracked drilling terms. "Trenchless directional drilling east rockaway" moved from 15.7 to 6.5, and "directional drilling vernon valley" from 31.5 to 21.9.

Wrong market

About 85% of drilling impressions (Mar to Sep) come from Long Island town searches such as Bayville, Plandome and East Hills. Google appears to be matching "Rockaway, NJ" to East Rockaway, NY. These impressions will not turn into NJ jobs.

Cannibalized

For the core local term "directional drilling in rockaway, nj" (261 impressions), Google shows the homepage via the GBP listing at position 1.0, not the service page.

Linking gap

The service page links to zero blog posts. Five of the blog links pointing to it use off-topic anchors like "sewer repair in Chatham, NJ".

Monthly trend

Impressions peaked in June and July, then fell back in August and September while average position improved. That pattern usually means distant, low-ranking Long Island queries are dropping out, which lifts the average without adding local reach.

Working

The Cost post is the only drilling page pulling repeat organic visits. Its 3 organic sessions averaged about 6.5 minutes, the strongest engagement in the cluster.

Low conversion share

The site logged 51 phone clicks and 14 thank-you page key events in the current 90 days. The drilling cluster produced 1 of them. The organic visitor who called came from Fredericksburg per GA4's IP location, which is often imprecise on mobile.

Dirty data

Only 18 of 40 users on drilling pages this period were in the US. 11 were in the Philippines and 7 in Singapore. Across six months, New Jersey accounted for just 10 users. Most of this is likely internal team visits, bots or data-center traffic, and it inflates the view counts above.

Traffic spike explained

Last period's 24 landing sessions on the service page included 18 from an Email channel with 2 engaged sessions and a 2-second average. That reads as link-scanner or bot traffic, not customers, so the drop to 2 landings this period is not a real loss.

Blog views mostly come from internal navigation, since organic landings are far lower than views. A truncated URL (/the-physics-behind-directional-drilling-steering-underground-with-precisio/) also recorded a view in the prior period, matching the truncated title tag; confirm it redirects to the full URL.

Working

For local intent, AI tools already treat RDH as the default directional drilling option around Rockaway and Morris County. Google AI Mode leans on the Business Profile, while ChatGPT and Perplexity read the service page.

Not cited

For cost and comparison questions, AI answers mostly quote per-foot price ranges and side-by-side tables from HomeGuide, Reddit and contractor sites. ChatGPT did cite the RDH cost post once. For lawn and driveway damage, ChatGPT used a JWSR LLC blog on boring under NJ driveways instead of the RDH Disturbance post. The RDH cost post does list per-foot ranges ($15 to $30 for small gas lines, $40 to $60 for 4 to 8 inch water lines, $35 to $120 for sewer lines), but only in running text with no table, and the trenching post has no cost figures at all.

Entity mismatch

ChatGPT labels every RDH citation "Rdhplumbingandhvacr.com" because the website's og:site_name tag and footer copyright still use that old domain, which no longer loads. ChatGPT's map card also shows (908) 344-3219 while the site uses (908) 578-9719, and Google AI Mode calls the business "RDH Plumbing & Specialist". Mixed name, phone and domain signals make AI tools less confident about the business.

GBP gap

Google AI Mode pulls RDH from the Business Profile, where the category is "Plumber" and the description mentions burst pipes and blocked drains. Adding directional drilling to the profile gives AI Mode a direct source for the service.

AI answers change by user, location and day. Tests were run once per prompt from a single browser with location unset, so treat this as a snapshot rather than a ranking.

Indexing status

What is working

  • All 8 URLs return "Submitted and indexed" with a PASS verdict.
  • Google's chosen canonical matches the declared canonical on every page. No duplicates or redirects.
  • Robots.txt allows crawling; all pages fetch successfully and are crawled as mobile.
  • Service page was recrawled Sep 19, 2026.

Issues found

  • Rich results FAIL on the service page: Video markup is missing the required thumbnailUrl field.
  • Wrong schema sitewide: every drilling page carries a Product snippet named "boiler, water heaters" and Review snippets tied to it.
  • Breadcrumb markup reports an "Unnamed item" on all 8 pages.
  • Stale crawls: the Physics post (Jun 29) and Top Applications (Jul 8) have not been recrawled since before the July posts that should link to them.
  • Sitemap index shows 1 warning and was last submitted Mar 2024; it is still being read (last downloaded Sep 16, 2026).

No page in the Directional Drilling cluster is excluded from the index. The indexing issues above are schema and crawl-freshness problems, not coverage problems.

Internal linking

Links flow one way and use the wrong words. The blogs send some authority to the service page, but most anchors describe sewer repair, and the service page sends nothing back.

Blogs to service page

  • 6 of 7 posts link to /directional-drilling/ in the body. The Disturbance post only reaches it through the main menu.
  • Only 2 anchors are on-topic ("directional drilling" on Physics and Cost).
  • 5 anchors describe sewer repair in a town ("sewer repair in Chatham, NJ", "Wharton", "Rockaway", "sewer repair services", "sewer line replacement"). This tells Google the page is about sewer repair and blurs it with /sewer-repair/.

Service page and cluster links

  • The service page links to 0 drilling blog posts. It mentions pipe bursting with no link.
  • Blog to blog: Disturbance links to Trenching and Cost; Physics links to Garden; Garden links to Top Applications. The rest rely on previous/next navigation.
  • Garden links to Top Applications with the anchor "replace sewer line", and Trenching links to an insurance post with the same anchor.
